Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy outlines how Tebo Creek Lodge, Sports Bar & Grill collects, uses, and safeguards information when you visit our website or interact with our services.

Effective Date: November 5, 2026

Policy Details

📋 1. Overview & Business Information

This Privacy Policy applies to the website https://tebocreeklodge.net (the “Site”) and services provided by Tebo Creek Lodge, Sports Bar & Grill (“we,” “us,” or “our”), located at 293 SE Hwy PP, Clinton, MO 64735.

We operate a hospitality, dining, and recreational lodging destination located near Truman Lake and Windsor Crossing boat ramp. We are committed to transparency and maintaining high standards of data protection and digital privacy compliance.

🔍 2. Information We Collect

We may collect information in two main ways:

A. Information You Voluntarily Provide

  • Telephone & In-Person Inquiries: When you call us at (660) 477-7777 for room reservations, food takeout orders, event questions, or lake boat parking inquiries, you may provide your name, telephone number, dates of stay, and order preferences.
  • Room Reservations: To protect guest privacy and credit security, room reservations are handled directly via telephone or in person rather than through third-party web tracking portals.
  • Email & Direct Messages: If you reach out to us directly, we retain your message and contact details to provide customer support.

B. Automatically Collected Information

  • Technical Data: When you navigate our Site, our web servers and analytics tools may automatically record standard device telemetry, including Internet Protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, operating system, language preferences, and referring URLs.
  • Usage & Interaction Data: Pages viewed, time spent on pages, links clicked, and general navigation flow across the Site.
  • Approximate Location: Non-precise regional location (such as city or state level derived from IP) used to deliver geographically relevant information regarding Truman Lake access and local driving directions.

⚙️ 3. How We Use Collected Information

We use collected data solely for legitimate business operations, including:

  • Service Fulfillment: Preparing takeout food orders, managing motel room accommodations, and providing requested information.
  • Customer Support: Responding to guest questions regarding kitchen hours, boat ramp proximity, menu items, and entertainment.
  • Website Performance & Optimization: Monitoring site responsiveness, Core Web Vitals, mobile usability, and server reliability.
  • Security & Fraud Prevention: Protecting our website, facilities, and patrons against unauthorized access, cyber incidents, and malicious activity.
  • Legal & Regulatory Compliance: Adhering to applicable local, state, and federal laws and hospitality regulations.

🍪 4. Cookies, Analytics & Web Technologies

Our Site utilizes standard web technologies, including cookies and Google Analytics, to provide a fast, responsive user experience:

  • Strictly Necessary Cookies: Essential for site performance, asset caching, video playback, and security.
  • Performance & Analytics Cookies: We use Google Analytics 4 (GA4) (Measurement ID: G-1B2RLSZ0Q9) and Google Tag Manager. These tools compile anonymous, aggregated statistics to help us understand which sections (such as our menu or lodging options) are most helpful to lake visitors.

Managing & Opting Out: You can configure your web browser to refuse all or some browser cookies, or to alert you when cookies are being sent. If you prefer to opt out of Google Analytics tracking across all websites, you can install the official Google Analytics Opt-Out Browser Add-on.

🛡️ 5. We Do Not Sell or Share Personal Information

We do not sell, rent, monetize, or trade your personal information. We do not share your personal information with third parties for cross-context behavioral advertising, data brokerage, or commercial marketing lists.

Information is only shared under limited circumstances:

  • Authorized Service Providers: Trusted technical vendors (such as web hosting and analytics infrastructure) who assist in site delivery under strict confidentiality obligations.
  • Legal Requirements: When required by lawful subpoena, court order, or law enforcement request in compliance with applicable law.
  • Protection of Rights: When necessary to protect the rights, property, safety, or security of Tebo Creek Lodge, our guests, employees, or the public.

💳 6. Payment Processing & PCI-DSS Compliance

Food orders and motel bookings made in person or over the telephone are processed using standard, encrypted, PCI-DSS compliant point-of-sale terminals.

We do not collect or store full payment card numbers on our website servers.

⚖️ 7. U.S. State Privacy Rights (CCPA / CPRA & Other States)

Residents of several U.S. states, including California (California Consumer Privacy Act / California Privacy Rights Act, Cal. Civ. Code § 1798.100 et seq.), Colorado, Virginia, Texas, and others, may have specific rights regarding their personal data:

  • Right to Know / Access: You may request disclosure of the categories and specific pieces of personal information we have collected about you.
  • Right to Deletion: You may request deletion of personal information we maintain, subject to statutory business retention exceptions.
  • Right to Correction: You may request correction of inaccurate personal records.
  • Right to Opt-Out of Sale or Sharing: As stated above, Tebo Creek Lodge does not sell or share personal data for behavioral advertising.
  • Right to Non-Discrimination: We will never discriminate against you, deny goods or services, or charge varying rates for exercising your privacy rights.

To exercise any of these statutory rights, please contact us by phone at (660) 477-7777 or by mail at our Clinton, MO address listed below.

🌐 8. International Visitors & European Privacy (GDPR / UK GDPR)

For visitors accessing our site from the European Economic Area (EEA), United Kingdom, or Canada, our legal bases for processing personal information include legitimate business interests (maintaining website security, fulfilling customer inquiries) and compliance with applicable legal obligations.

You have the right to request access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, and data portability of your personal data under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), as well as the right to lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority.

👶 9. Children's Privacy (COPPA) & Age Restrictions

Our Site is designed for a general audience and is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 in accordance with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

Alcohol & Bar Facility Notice: Tebo's Sports Bar & Grill complies strictly with Missouri alcohol regulations. Patrons ordering alcoholic beverages, entering designated bar areas, or attending 21+ events must present valid, government-issued photo identification.

🔒 10. Data Security & Retention

We implement industry-standard technical and organizational security measures, including SSL/TLS encryption (HTTPS), firewalls, and restricted administrative access to protect personal data against loss, misuse, unauthorized access, or alteration.

We retain personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy, complete reservations or food orders, resolve disputes, and comply with tax and legal requirements.

🔗 11. Third-Party Websites & Links

Our Site contains links to third-party services for your convenience, such as Google Maps (for driving directions), Facebook, and Instagram (for community updates). We are not responsible for the privacy practices, content, or policies of third-party websites. We encourage you to review their respective privacy statements when leaving our Site.

🛑 12. Do Not Track & Global Privacy Control (GPC)

We respect browser-based opt-out preference signals, including Global Privacy Control (GPC), where required by applicable law. Because we do not track visitors across third-party websites or engage in behavioral advertising sales, our data collection practices are already minimal and privacy-focused by default.

🔄 13. Updates to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in our operational practices, services, or legal obligations. When changes are made, the “Effective Date” at the top of this page will be revised. We encourage visitors to review this page periodically.

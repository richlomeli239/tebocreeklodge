Truman Lake Fishing & Outdoor Amenities

Your basecamp for crappie, catfish, and bass fishing with dedicated amenities designed for anglers.

🎣

Premier Fishing Waters

Truman Lake is famous nationwide for its incredible timbered coves and monster crappie, trophy blue catfish, largemouth bass, and hybrid stripers.

  • ✔️ Crappie: Top-tier brush pile fishing year-round.
  • ✔️ Catfish: Deep channel flats yielding trophy blues & flatheads.
  • ✔️ Bass: Standing timber and rocky point action.
⚓

Angler Designed Facilities

We know what fishermen need when coming off the water. Tebo Creek Lodge provides all the essential amenities for your rig and catch.

  • 🔌 Outdoor Outlets: Charge your troll batteries overnight right by your cabin.
  • 🐟 Cleaning Station: On-site access to clean your daily limit.
  • 🚤 Trailer Parking: Oversized lot built for big trucks and bass boats.

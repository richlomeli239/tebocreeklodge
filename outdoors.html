Truman Lake Outdoors Guide

Tebo Creek Lodge sits right in the heart of Missouri's premier fishing and hunting territory. Here is what you need to know before hitting the water or woods.

🎣 Target Fishing Species at Truman Lake

Crappie

Truman Lake is nationally famous for black and white crappie. Target standing timber, brush piles, and bridge pilings with jigs and live minnows.

Peak: Spring & Fall

Catfish (Blue, Channel & Flathead)

Trophy blue catfish thrive in the deep channels and flats. Drift fishing with cut bait yields massive catches throughout the warmer months.

Peak: Late Spring & Summer

Bass & Hybrid Stripers

Largemouth bass hold tight to shallow wood and rock points. Hybrid striped bass provide fast-paced, hard-hitting action in open waters.

Peak: Spring & Early Summer

🚤 Boat Ramp & Water Access

Tebo Creek Lodge provides quick access to multiple public boat ramps on the Tebo arm and main body of Truman Lake, reducing trailer travel time.

  • ✓ Tebo Creek Arm Access: Minutes from our parking lot for fast launching on calm waters.
  • ✓ On-Site Amenities: Electric hookups outside your cabin to charge batteries overnight.
  • ✓ Fish Cleaning Station: Prep your catch right at the lodge before heading into the bar.

🦌 Hunting in Henry County & Truman Reservoir

Thousands of acres of U.S. Army Corps of Engineers public land surround Truman Lake, providing prime opportunities for deer, turkey, and waterfowl hunting.

  • ✓ Whitetail Deer: Exceptional archery and firearm deer hunting across surrounding public management areas.
  • ✓ Spring & Fall Turkey: Strong bird populations throughout the hardwood timber ridges.
  • ✓ Waterfowl: Marsh and creek arms draw duck and goose hunters all winter long.

Need Missouri Fishing & Hunting Licenses?

Make sure you have your current Missouri Department of Conservation (MDC) permits and check lake levels before heading out.
